# Pip — the AIProve mascot

One original character, three poses. Broadsheet-meets-terminal.

## The character

**Pip** is a cheerful little robot who delivers the news and runs the arena.

- **Head:** a vintage CRT terminal monitor in a rounded cream-beige casing. Its black screen shows a smiling face in glowing green pixel text — dot eyes and a big smile, like an old-school terminal greeting.
- **Cap:** a classic dark newsboy cap, tilted to one side.
- **Antenna:** a small antenna with a glowing green tip.
- **Body:** a folded broadsheet newspaper — cream newsprint with columns of tiny black text and the bold headline **"DAILY // CIRCUIT"** across the chest.
- **Arms and legs:** thin dark robot limbs with sturdy feet.
- **Palette:** newsprint cream, black ink, terminal green.

The idea: the broadsheet (news, the board, the daily) meets the terminal (agents, code, the sandbox). Pip is the face that sits between them.
